== Message examples

The samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (directory `samples`).

=== strong-wind.xml – strong wind

A warning of the road weather station Cínovec 2 of strong wind (snapshot of 30{nbsp}September 2026).

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/strong-wind.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of type `sit:SituationPublication` with the attributes `@profileName` and `@profileVersion`,
`publicationTime` and `publicationCreator`. `feedType` refers to the distribution (data source).

Situation::
The situation contains one record of type `sit:PoorEnvironmentConditions` in version 4: strong wind (`strongWinds`). The
record was created at 0:07:31 and renewed three times until 0:27:31; the originator is the road weather stations
(`ITSMETEO`).

Validity::
The current version is valid for 20 minutes – from 0:21 to 0:41 (`overallStartTime`, `overallEndTime`).

Location::
A reference to the predefined section of the road weather station Cínovec 2 (`loc:LocationByReference`,
`@targetClass` = `loc:PredefinedLocation`) with the identifier `88410412-1bf1-4ace-8e84-66cb60d127db` in version 260601; the section
is in the sample `predefined_location_set-weather.xml` of the format `cz-ndic_d2-pls`.

=== strong-wind-loc_group.xml – strong wind for a group of places

An illustrative variant of the message from the sample `strong-wind.xml` (with a different situation identifier) whose location refers to a group of predefined locations (`loc:LocationGroupByReference`,
`@targetClass` = `loc:PredefinedLocationGroup`). The referenced group is the sample
`predefined_location_loc_group.xml` of the format `cz-ndic_d2-pls`. A reference to a group does not yet occur in
production data; the sample only shows how it is written.

=== aquaplaning.xml – risk of aquaplaning

A continuously updated message about an imminent risk of aquaplaning.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/aquaplaning.xml[tags=**]
----

Record::
Type `sit:WeatherRelatedRoadConditions` with the value `surfaceWater` (water on the road surface). The
phenomenon is imminent, hence `probabilityOfOccurrence` = `riskOf`.

Version and validity::
The record was created at 0:01 (`situationRecordCreationTime`) and by 21:48 it had been updated to
version 212. Each version is valid for 20 minutes – this one from 21:46 to 22:06.

Location::
Reference to a predefined road section in version 260601; the section description is contained in the
publication of the `cz-ndic_d2-pls` format.
